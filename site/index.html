FROM FIRST WORD TO FINISHED DRAFT

Make yourself at home.

  1. 01

    Bring the original

    Load your extracted Japanese script.tsv in the Studio to read it alongside the English draft. Your source file stays in your browser.

  2. 02

    Find the right words

    Edit the draft, watch the game-font preview, and check line lengths, spaces and control codes as you write. Drafts save on this device.

  3. 03

    Take your work with you

    Download a TSV of your validated changes. Reopen it in the Studio later, or bring it into the project for review and game testing.

The current game font supports the project’s English glyph set. Other writing systems need additional font support.
